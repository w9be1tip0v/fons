## Abstract / Summary
OpenAI argues teens need AI access with age-appropriate protections rather than delayed access until adulthood. Updates include stronger default teen protections, age prediction, parental controls (including Study Mode defaults), break reminders, family resources, education starter prompts, and parent notifications when linked teen accounts are deactivated for violent-threat policy violations. Guided by four commitments: teen safety first, real-world support in need, treat teens as teens, transparency. Partners include AFT, APA, Expert Council on Well-Being and AI, FOSI, Common Sense Media, and open-sourced teen safety policies/tools for developers.
