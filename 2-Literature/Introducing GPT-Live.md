# Introducing GPT-Live

## Source Metadata
- type:: blog
- source:: [OpenAI News](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-live)
- published:: 2026-07-08

## Abstract / Summary
OpenAI is launching GPT-Live, a new generation of full-duplex voice models that listen and speak at the same time, powering a more natural ChatGPT Voice experience. GPT-Live can backchannel, handle rapid turn-taking, stay silent when appropriate, and delegate search, deeper reasoning, or complex work to a frontier model (GPT-5.5 at launch) while continuing the conversation. Two versions—GPT-Live-1 and GPT-Live-1 mini—are rolling out globally on ChatGPT, with API access planned. The post contrasts cascaded STT-LLM-TTS and turn-based Advanced Voice Mode with continuous interaction plus background delegation, arguing this architecture unlocks longer-running and more agentic voice workflows over time.
