## Abstract / Summary
Multimodal LLMs are increasingly used to interpret visualizations, but evaluations stay chart-centric and under-test scientific visualization (SciVis). This work benchmarks six MLLMs on the SciVis literacy assessment test: 49 items from 18 scientific visualizations/illustrations, 8 techniques and 11 task types, versus 485 human participants. Literacy is uneven: Gemini is strongest and exceeds the human mean on evaluated subsets; open-source models stay below the human baseline. Models do better on scientific illustration, search, and spatial understanding, and struggle on texture-based and integration-based visualizations and quantitative estimation. Recurring errors include fine-grained quantitative estimation, flow-direction interpretation, and grounded encoding. Positions SciVis literacy as a necessary multimodal eval axis.
