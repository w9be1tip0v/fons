## Abstract / Summary
Google DeepMind announces two generative-media releases for builders: Nano Banana 2 Lite, described as the fastest and most cost-efficient Gemini Image model in the Nano Banana family for high-throughput image generation, and Gemini Omni Flash for high-quality video generation and conversational multi-turn editing. Both are available via Google AI Studio, Gemini API, and Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform, with consumer surface rollouts including the Gemini app, AI Mode in Search, and Google Flow. The post positions the pair as an end-to-end path from rapid image iteration to video creation and editing for scalable creative workflows.
