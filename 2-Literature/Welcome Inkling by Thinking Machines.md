## Abstract / Summary
Inkling by Thinking Machines is released on Hugging Face as a large (~1T parameter) open multimodal LLM that natively accepts image, text, and audio, with agentic capabilities and 1M context. It ships in full BF16 and a calibrated NVFP4 variant, includes speculative MTP layers for faster inference, and has day-0 support in transformers, SGLang, and llama.cpp. The post highlights modality coverage and deployment readiness as the main differentiators.
