## Abstract / Summary
Retrieval-Augmented Generation is widely used to reduce hallucinations, but the impact of ideological bias in retrieved material is underexplored. Using Lexical Multidimensional Analysis on 1,117 COVID-19 treatment articles as the RAG knowledge source, the authors identify three ideological discourses and test several LLMs answering ideological questions at different sampling temperatures. Findings: RAG can transfer ideological discourses into responses; discursive alignment peaks at moderate temperatures and drops at low temperatures where overly deterministic sampling suppresses discourse transfer.
