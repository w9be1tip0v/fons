## Abstract / Summary
IBM Research discusses model routing for multi-model LLM stacks. Routing looks simple—send easy queries to cheap models and hard ones to stronger models—but production constraints around cost, latency, quality drift, and operational complexity make robust routing non-trivial. The post targets enterprise multi-model deployment patterns rather than a single-model chatbot setup.
