## Abstract / Summary
The authors present a theoretical framework for emergence of inductive reasoning in Transformer language models. They study a generalized class of inductive tasks unifying in-context n-grams and multi-hop reasoning, and prove that training dynamics of attention models can be confined to a highly interpretable low-dimensional invariant manifold. On that manifold, learning is captured by a handful of coordinates, enabling analysis of in-context vs in-weights competition, circuit selection under random initialization, and automatic detection of learned circuits.
