## Abstract / Summary
Paper figure edits (relabel, rearrange, restyle) are routine but hard to automate under natural-language instructions because scientific figures are dense multi-element infographics. SciDiagramEdit is a benchmark and skill-evolution framework that learns from natural paper revisions and operates on editable vector sources so users can inspect/co-edit primitives with the agent. Before/after figure pairs are mined from arXiv version histories, each grounded in authors' revision intent. An agentic proposer refines the agent's skill specification from execution traces over multiple epochs; the evolved skill lifts edit accuracy on held-out validation, showing natural revisions are a useful training signal for instruction-driven figure editing.
