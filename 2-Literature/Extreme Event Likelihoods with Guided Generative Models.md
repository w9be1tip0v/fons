Across science, engineering, and finance, many critical risks are low-likelihood high-impact events. Brute-force Monte Carlo can require excessive model iterations when each sample is expensive. This post discusses estimating extreme-event likelihoods with guided generative models as a more sample-efficient alternative for rare-outcome probability estimation.
