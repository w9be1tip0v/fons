## Abstract / Summary
Cars24 (India/UAE/Australia used-car marketplace) uses OpenAI APIs for voice and chat agents across buy/sell/finance/support funnels, handling 1M+ monthly conversation minutes. Reported outcomes: 50% increase in support resolution rates, 80% reduction in turnaround on key service workflows, 12% recovery of previously lost seller leads. Internally, ChatGPT Enterprise and Codex reach ~600 central employees with 85–90% daily active use; Codex is embedded in Linear/GitHub workflows and non-engineering teams build finance, IR, and “chief of staff” agents. Case study of agents as both customer engagement and internal operating layer.
