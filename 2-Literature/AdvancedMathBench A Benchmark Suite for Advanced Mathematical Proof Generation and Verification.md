## Abstract / Summary
Large language models have strong high-school and olympiad-style math performance, but advanced mathematics remains poorly understood. AdvancedMathBench evaluates advanced mathematical reasoning with ProverBench (296 undergraduate and doctoral qualifying-exam proof problems) and a dedicated automatic verification pipeline trained on expert annotations. VerifierBench contains 888 model-generated proof trajectories with expert ground truth. Best models still leave substantial headroom: GPT-5.5-xhigh reaches 75.8/66.1 on UGD/QE splits; best verification Balanced F1 is only 65.1, with low true-negative rates indicating weak critical error detection.
