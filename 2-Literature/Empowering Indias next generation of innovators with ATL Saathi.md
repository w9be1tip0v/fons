## Abstract / Summary
Google DeepMind and Atal Innovation Mission (NITI Aayog) launch a live pilot of ATL Saathi, a Gemini-powered web app giving Tinkering Lab educators a 24/7 planning and training assistant. Features include NotebookLM-backed module summaries, quizzes, and Gemini 3.5 Flash generation of grade-appropriate curriculum-aligned project ideas with assembly instructions. Initial rollout targets 100 pilot schools, aiming to cut administrative load so teachers can mentor students.
