## Abstract / Summary
Sarah Friar argues enterprise AI value should be measured as useful intelligence per dollar, not seats or cost-per-token. Four questions: (1) how much useful work is completed; (2) full cost per successful task including retries and human review; (3) dependability (ready / needs correction / needs escalation); (4) whether value improves as usage scales. Positions GPT-5.6 Sol/Terra/Luna tiers as starting points optimized for outcome economics, citing coding-agent efficiency gains. Ties compute, dependability, and product design to compounding ROI.
