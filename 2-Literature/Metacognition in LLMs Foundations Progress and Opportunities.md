## Abstract / Summary
Metacognition is a foundational component of intelligence critical to effective learning, problem solving, decision-making, and communication. It is increasingly recognized as a cornerstone of capable, transparent AI systems. Yet while LLMs have made significant progress across diverse real-world tasks, it is not yet clear when, how, or to what extent they can exhibit or be endowed with effective metacognitive abilities, nor how such abilities can be adapted to advance fundamental capabilities, reliability, and intelligence. This paper presents the first comprehensive overview of the current state of knowledge on metacognition for LLMs, including methods and benchmarks to measure and evaluate metacognitive abilities, techniques to elicit, improve, and apply metacognition, and open challenges. An organized list of papers is at https://github.com/yale-nlp/LLM-Metacognition.
