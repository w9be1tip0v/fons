# Scaling Agentic AI Factories Through Extreme Co-Design with NVIDIA BlueField

## Source Metadata

## Abstract / Summary
Agentic AI turns inference into distributed workflows of model calls, tools, memory, policy, storage, and network hops—making infrastructure part of the inference path. NVIDIA BlueField-4 DPUs and Vera BlueField-4 STX storage processors, with DOCA, offload/accelerate/isolate networking, storage, security, telemetry, and context (including KV cache placement/reuse) from host CPUs. BlueField-4: up to 800 Gb/s Eth/IB, 64-core Grace CPU, PCIe Gen6, LPDDR5X. Aimed at higher GPU utilization, predictable latency, tenant isolation, lower cost/token and more tokens/watt in Vera Rubin / DSX AI factories.
