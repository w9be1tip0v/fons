## Abstract / Summary
Google DeepMind and Isomorphic Labs publish a joint approach to bioresilience: prevent threat actors from misusing models, and ensure governments, scientists, and biosecurity experts can harness frontier AI for a more resilient response to outbreaks. Over 12 months they advanced more than 15 partnerships across prevention, detection, and response, citing AlphaFold, IsoDDE, and AlphaGenome as tools that shift work from pure reaction toward proactive defense and faster therapeutic discovery. Models and agents are made available to trusted partners under these program goals.
