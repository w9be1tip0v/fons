# Securing the future of AI agents

## Source Metadata

## Abstract / Summary
Google DeepMind describes an AI Control Roadmap for securing increasingly capable internal AI agents when alignment may be imperfect. The approach layers traditional cybersecurity (sandboxing, endpoint security, prompt-injection resistance) with model alignment and system-level control that treats untrusted agents like potential insider threats, drawing on MITRE ATT&CK-style threat modeling. Mitigations emphasize detection via trusted supervisor models that monitor reasoning and actions, plus prevention/response that can block harmful steps. Success is measured by coverage, recall of misaligned behavior, and time-to-response. The post also points to a policy-oriented 'Three Layers of Agent Security' framework spanning individual agents, multi-agent systems, and broader cyber-defense resilience.
